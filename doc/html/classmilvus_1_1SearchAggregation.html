Detailed Description

A search aggregation over one or more group-by fields, with metrics, ordering, top hits and nesting.

Used by
MilvusClientV2::Search() via SearchRequest::WithSearchAggregation().

Not combinable with a non-zero offset, hybrid search, search/query iterators, a highlighter, or grouping search. Supports at most four aggregation levels and at most ten bucket-key fields in total.
